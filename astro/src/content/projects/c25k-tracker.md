A fully self-contained interval run tracker built for the Couch to 5K program. Choose any week's workout, hit Start, and the app walks you through every jog and walk interval with voice announcements, audio tones, and an optional woodblock metronome to keep your cadence sharp.

Features include:

- **Full C25K library** — all 9 weeks of workouts pre-loaded
- **Custom workout builder** — define your own jog/walk segments
- **Voice cues** — speech synthesis announces each phase transition
- **Metronome** — adjustable BPM woodblock click for cadence training
- **Screen wake lock** — screen stays on during your run
- **Offline-capable** — no network needed once loaded
- **Progress tracking** — completed workouts are marked in the library

Works best when added to your home screen as a PWA.
